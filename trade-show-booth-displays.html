Booth Display FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

These are the questions most businesses should answer before requesting a trade show booth display quote.

What should be included in a trade show booth kit?

Most small booths start with a backdrop, branded table cover, banner stand, counter or display table, and a simple literature rack or sign holder.

How do I know what display pieces I need?

Start with booth size, event type, what you need visitors to do, and what you already own. Say It can help you avoid buying pieces that do not support the goal.

Can Say It design the full booth graphics?

Yes. Send your logo, brand colors, website, product photos, and any existing print files. We can help make the booth feel coordinated before proof approval.

Do you help with booth displays in Tucson and Arizona?

Yes. Say It helps Tucson, Phoenix, Arizona, and nationwide businesses quote booth displays, prepare artwork, review proofs, and order around event deadlines.
